# C329 — Generative AI for Business Presentation

Build a polished business presentation end to end with AI. This one-day, hands-on course teaches you to
use generative AI — general chat assistants (**ChatGPT**, **Claude**, **Gemini**) and an **AI slide
generator** (Gamma, Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint, Canva) — across the entire presentation workflow:
researching the topic and audience, shaping a persuasive message and story, outlining the deck,
generating slide content, designing professional slides, creating charts, images and speaker notes,
and rehearsing and delivering with confidence — while keeping the story, the facts and the delivery
firmly your own.

## One Connected Project

Every lab builds one **connected deliverable** — the **Meridian Fresh pitch deck**, a business
presentation for a fictional Singapore healthy meal-kit startup seeking approval to expand into three
new neighbourhoods. You start in Lab 1 by setting up an AI presentation toolkit and prompt library, and
across the day take the deck all the way to a rehearsed, exported presentation by Lab 10: fact-checked
research, a clear message and story, a slide-by-slide outline, concise slide copy, a professionally
designed deck, honest charts, on-brand images, per-slide speaker notes, and a delivery cheat-sheet.
A Meridian Fresh brief with the facts and figures is supplied in [`labs/reference-pack/`](labs/reference-pack/).

## Topics

1. **Getting Started with Generative AI for Presentations** — introduction to generative AI for business
   presentations; popular GenAI tools (ChatGPT, Claude and AI slide generators); writing effective
   prompts for presentations; structuring your message and story. *(Labs 1–5)*
2. **Designing and Delivering AI-Powered Presentations** — generating slide content and visuals;
   designing professional slides with AI; creating charts, images and speaker notes; rehearsing and
   delivering with confidence. *(Labs 6–10)*
