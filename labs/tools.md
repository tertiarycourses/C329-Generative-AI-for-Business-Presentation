# Tools & Setup — Generative AI for Business Presentation (C329)

Everything you need for the 10 hands-on labs. Every tool used in this course is
**web-based and cloud-powered** — there is nothing to install beyond a modern
browser. You use two kinds of tool together: **general chat assistants** for the
thinking, research and words, and an **AI slide generator** for the design.

## Accounts & software

| Tool | What it's for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Web browser** (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox) | Runs every tool in this course | Keep it up to date |
| **A chat assistant** — ChatGPT (chat.openai.com), Claude (claude.ai) or Gemini (gemini.google.com) | Research, story, outline, slide copy, speaker notes, Q&A rehearsal | A free account for any one of them is enough; the trainer will confirm what's available |
| **An AI slide generator** — Gamma (gamma.app) | Turns your outline/copy into a designed deck | Used in the labs; a free Gamma account works. Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint or Canva are fine alternatives |
| **An image tool** — DALL·E in ChatGPT, or the image features in Gamma / Canva | Generating on-brand images for slides (Lab 9) | Any one of these is enough |
| **Internet connection** | Every generative feature runs in the cloud | Required for every step |

## What each tool does in this course

| Tool | What you do with it | Labs |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini** | Draft prompts, research the topic and audience, shape the message and story, outline the deck, write slide copy and speaker notes, rehearse Q&A | 1–6, 8–10 |
| **Gamma** (AI slide generator) | Generate a designed deck from your outline, apply a theme, refine layout | 1, 7 |
| **A chart tool** (Gamma / Canva / Excel / Google Sheets) | Build the data chart from the brief's figures | 8 |
| **An image tool** (DALL·E / Gamma / Canva) | Generate on-brand slide images | 9 |

## The AI workflow you'll use

Every lab follows the same loop — **prompt → review → refine** — and the course
as a whole follows one workflow:

**Research → Structure → Generate → Refine → Deliver.**

You supply and verify the real facts; the AI drafts, designs and rehearses; you
own every claim and the delivery.

## Before Lab 1 — confirm your setup

1. Open and **sign in** to at least one chat assistant (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini).
2. Open and **sign in** to an AI slide generator (**Gamma**).
3. Send a simple prompt (for example *"Hello, are you ready to help me build a business presentation?"*) and confirm you get a reply.
4. Create a project folder called **`Meridian-Fresh-Deck`** for all your work.
5. Open the **Meridian Fresh brief** in [reference-pack/](reference-pack/).

If any of these is missing, tell the trainer at the start of the day.

## Responsible & honest use

- Do **not** paste confidential company data, personal information or unpublished financials into a public AI tool — use the supplied Meridian Fresh brief.
- Treat every AI output — **especially facts, figures and charts** — as a first draft to fact-check. Use the brief's real numbers, never AI-invented ones.
- Be transparent about AI assistance (including AI-generated images) where appropriate before a presentation goes to a real audience.

## The connected project

Every lab builds one part of the **Meridian Fresh pitch deck** (see
[reference-pack/](reference-pack/)). Keep everything in your `Meridian-Fresh-Deck`
folder so the presentation stays together. There is **no assessment** — each lab
verifies itself with a **Test it** step.
