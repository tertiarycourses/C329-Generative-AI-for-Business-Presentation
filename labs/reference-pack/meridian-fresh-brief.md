# Meridian Fresh — Presentation Brief

> **This is a fictional company created for the C329 course.** All names, figures
> and details are invented for practice. Use this brief as the source material for
> your pitch deck, or substitute your own **non-confidential** presentation.

## The company in one line

**Meridian Fresh** delivers chef-designed, ready-to-cook **healthy meal kits** to
busy professionals in Singapore — fresh ingredients, pre-portioned, with a
20-minute recipe card, delivered weekly.

## The story so far

- Founded **two years ago**, operating from a single central kitchen in **Tanjong Pagar**, serving that one neighbourhood.
- Started with 3 weekly menus; now offers 12 rotating menus including vegetarian, high-protein and low-carb lines.
- Grew almost entirely by word of mouth and a small local social-media budget.
- After two strong years, the single kitchen and delivery zone are near capacity — growth in one neighbourhood has plateaued.

## The opportunity

- Demand from **outside Tanjong Pagar** is strong: the waitlist for delivery to other areas has grown steadily, and the team turns away enquiries every week.
- Busy urban professionals across Singapore face the same problem: they want to eat healthily but lack the time to plan, shop and cook.
- Meridian Fresh believes the model will repeat in three new neighbourhoods with the right kitchen capacity and local marketing.

## The proposal (what the presentation is asking for)

Expand Meridian Fresh into **three new neighbourhoods** — **Tampines, Jurong East
and Punggol** — over **12 months**, by adding satellite kitchen capacity, delivery
routes, and local marketing, and by hiring a small operations and kitchen team.

**The ask:** approval of a **S$450,000 expansion budget** and the 12-month,
three-neighbourhood rollout plan.

## The audience

The **quarterly strategy review**: the Meridian Fresh **leadership team** (the two
co-founders, the head of operations and the head of marketing) plus **one visiting
investor** who is considering a follow-on investment.

- **They care about:** sustainable growth, unit economics, execution risk, and whether the model really repeats beyond the first neighbourhood.
- **They already know:** the product and the first-neighbourhood story.
- **Their likely concerns:** can the kitchen and delivery scale? will retention hold in new areas? is S$450,000 the right amount, and what does it buy?

## The expansion plan (high level)

| Phase | Months | Neighbourhood | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–4 | Tampines | Satellite kitchen, first delivery routes, launch marketing |
| 2 | 5–8 | Jurong East | Second kitchen, repeat the Tampines playbook |
| 3 | 9–12 | Punggol | Third kitchen, optimise routes across all four areas |

## The roadmap after expansion

- End of Year 3: four neighbourhoods live, targeting **~5,300 total active subscribers**.
- Introduce a **corporate/office** weekly plan.
- Explore a **cook-from-frozen** range to widen the delivery window.

## Suggested narrative (you will shape your own in Lab 4)

Situation (busy Singaporeans want to eat well but have no time) → Complication
(one-neighbourhood growth has plateaued while demand elsewhere is unmet) →
Solution (a proven meal-kit model, ready to repeat) → Proof (two years of steady
growth and healthy unit economics — see the fact sheet) → Ask (approve the
S$450,000, three-neighbourhood, 12-month expansion).
